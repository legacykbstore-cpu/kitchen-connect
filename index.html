How this version works

Open targeted public-conversation searches, verify that a post is from a local homeowner, then save its link and details. Search results are not automatically imported or verified. Lead scores are based on your entries, not verified identities. No automated outreach is sent.

Find homeowner discussions

Search tip

Look for first-person questions from residents, not service listings. Public search engines may return few or no genuine local posts. Never assume someone is a homeowner based on a keyword match.
